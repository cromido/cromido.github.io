# <span class="cromido-display">CroMiDo</span>

## Cash donations to people in need

<div>

When you donate to charity, do you know how much of your money reaches its destination? Do you know the impact of your donation? Do you feel that your small donations have little impact?

<span class="cromido-display">CroMiDo</span> steps in to help out. We use mobile technology to create transparency, allowing you to donate small sums directly to the people who need it most. With one click you can make someone’s day on the other side of the world!
